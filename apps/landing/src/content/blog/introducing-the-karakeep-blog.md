Karakeep has shipped more than 50 releases since the first version in February 2024. Until now, the only place to read about them was GitHub. Starting today, they also live here, on the Karakeep blog.

## What you'll find here

- **Release notes.** [Every release](/blog/releases/) since 0.11.0, with new features, fixes and upgrade notes. New releases show up here on the day they ship.
- **Guides.** Practical write-ups on getting more out of Karakeep: migrating from other read-it-later apps, self-hosting setups, AI tagging with local models, and automating your library with the API, CLI and MCP server.
- **Stories.** Milestones, design decisions, and what we're learning while building an open-source product in public.

## Follow along

The blog has an [RSS feed](/blog/rss.xml) that includes both articles and release notes. Karakeep can subscribe to RSS feeds itself, so you can add the feed to your own Karakeep and get new posts saved as bookmarks automatically.

If there's something you'd like us to write about, let us know on [GitHub](https://github.com/karakeep-app/karakeep/discussions) or [Discord](https://discord.gg/NrgeYywsFh).
