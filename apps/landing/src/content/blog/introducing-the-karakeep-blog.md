Karakeep has shipped more than 50 releases since the first version in February 2024. Until now, the release notes only lived on GitHub. Starting today, they have a home on karakeep.com too.

## What we're publishing here

- **Release notes.** [Every release since v0.11.0](/blog/releases/), with features, fixes, and upgrade notes. New releases will be posted here alongside GitHub.
- **Guides (coming soon).** Practical write-ups on getting more out of Karakeep: migrating from other read-it-later apps, self-hosting setups, AI tagging with local models, and automating your library with the API, CLI, and MCP server.
- **Stories (coming soon).** Milestones, design trade-offs, and behind-the-scenes notes on Karakeep.

## Follow along

The blog has an [RSS feed](/blog/rss.xml) that includes both articles and release notes. Karakeep can subscribe to RSS feeds itself, so you can add the feed to your Karakeep account and get new posts saved as bookmarks automatically.

If there's something you'd like us to write about, let us know on [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/karakeep-app/karakeep/discussions) or [Discord](https://discord.gg/NrgeYywsFh).
