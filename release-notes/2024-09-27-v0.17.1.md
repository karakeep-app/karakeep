This is fixed in https://github.com/hoarder-app/hoarder/commit/12d3371d12d5709adc6e23b53792cf1dc28a8a06 & https://github.com/hoarder-app/hoarder/commit/4db50c234fb0da8747b902a7c1e551cc54451f4c.
* A "name" is no longer required from SSO providers. @kamtschatka fixed that in https://github.com/hoarder-app/hoarder/commit/e1feeceda475b117dde99a971f87e20a149f7edf.
